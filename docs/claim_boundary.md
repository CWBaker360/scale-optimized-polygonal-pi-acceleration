# Claim Boundary

The authoritative paper proves scale cancellation, uniform geometric-node
weight stability, and an explicit analytic remainder bound.

It does not claim:

- that every scale branch is exactly compass-and-straightedge constructible;
- that the regular 18-gon or the ladder `6 -> 18 -> 54 -> ...` is exactly constructible;
- that odd symmetry alone forces a linear term to vanish;
- that one scale factor is universally optimal without a cost model;
- that the method is faster than AGM or modular-form algorithms;
- that Richardson extrapolation itself is new.
